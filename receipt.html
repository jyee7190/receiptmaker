how to use

  1. Pick what you are making: quotation, invoice or receipt.
  2. Fill in the date and reference number.
  3. Type your items. The totals add up by themselves.
  4. Tap + add new row if you need more lines.
  5. Press PNG or PDF to save your cute copy.
